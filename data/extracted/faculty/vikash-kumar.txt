Faculty at School of Humanities and Social Sciences

Biography
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dr. Vikash Kumar holds a Ph.D. from IIT Guwahati. He is currently an assistant professor in the Department of Design, School of Humanities and Social Sciences at Shiv Nadar University. Previously, he worked as a programmer analyst at Cognizant Technology Solutions India in Chennai. His research interests include product-service system design for sustainability, design pedagogy, social design, and innovation through sustainable design. Among his achievements, he served as an expert and jury member representing India in the Industrial Design Technology category at the Asia-Pacific Best Practice Marathon 2022 (IV Khabarovsk International Skills Competition) organized by the WorldSkills Russia Regional Centre. Currently, he is the Chief Expert of India Skill in the Industrial Design Technology category to nominate candidates for WorldSkills 2024. He has been invited by institutions like Delhi Technical University and NID, Kurukshetra, to mentor design students.
