Subhrajit Mukherjee

Department of Physics

Biography:
Dr. Subhrajit Mukherjee has been extensively working on the development of 2D material based solid-state quantum dots to realize spin-valley qubits. His research integrates cutting-edge semiconductor technology and quantum devices, with a strong focus on micro- and nanoscale fabrication. His research interests lie in the physics of semiconductor devices, reflected in numerous publications in high-impact academic journals, U.S. patents, and presentations at international conferences. Driven by fundamental curiosity, his work holds immense potential for future technological applications, including advancements in optically controlled integrated memory devices, synaptic devices, room-temperature lasing and quantum device engineering.

Research Interests:
In SNIoE, our research group (
Optoelectronic Materials & Device Lab
) explores the intersection of optoelectronics and neuromorphic engineering. We focus on developing bio-inspired, optoelectronically controlled devices for next-generation smart sensors, in-memory computation and storage hardware, and defense-related autonomous system. Our approach leverages the unique properties of 2D materials and their heterogeneous integration to drive innovation in future electronic and photonic technologies. At OEMD, our research spans graphene, TMDs, semimetals, ferroelectrics, and other emerging layered semiconductors, with a strong emphasis on understanding light-matter interactions, device physics and their underlying mechanisms.
