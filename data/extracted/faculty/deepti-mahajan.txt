Biography

Biography
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Deepti is a dedicated sustainability researcher currently pursuing her Ph.D. at Nottingham Trent University in the UK. Her research, focusing on transforming consumer goods markets through sustainable practices, is driven by a passionate commitment to building a more sustainable world.
Currently, Deepti is also actively involved with the Office of Dean of Academics at Shiv Nadar University where she delivers courses on fashion, consumption, sustainability, transformational leadership and Global Goals. Critical thinking and academic reflective writing are key inclusions in all her courses. She is also responsible for designing and delivering a university-wide sustainability ambassador program and promoting a collaborative writing environment.
Before her academic journey, Deepti made a tangible impact in the field of sustainability as a consultant with The Energy and Resource Institute (TERI) in New Delhi. Her MBA project in Business Sustainability that led to Cargill’s CSR policy, along with diverse roles at prominent organisations like Encyclopaedia Britannica, Google and Midas Touch Investors Association, a project of the Ministry of Company Affairs, demonstrates her well-rounded expertise and commitment to driving positive change towards a sustainable future.
