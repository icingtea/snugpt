Sujana Kabiraj
================================================================================

Faculty at School of Management and Entrepreneurship

Research Interest
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
My primary research interests include economic growth, regional economics, convergence, and the Indian economy. My current research focuses on growth and convergence in Indian districts using satellite night light data.
