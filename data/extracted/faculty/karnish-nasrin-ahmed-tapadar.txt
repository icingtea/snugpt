Faculty at School of Engineering

Biography
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Karnish Nasrin Ahmed Tapadar has done her Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati (IITG) under the supervision of Dr. Manas Khatua and Prof. Tamarapalli Venkatesh. She holds an M.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Durgapur, where she was awarded the Institute Gold Medal.
Her research focuses on Computer Networks, particularly IoT, 6TiSCH, IEEE 802.15.4e, and Security-by-Design in IoT, along with performance evaluation of communication protocols, wireless networks, and sensor networks. Her work has been published in reputed journals and conferences.
Karnish has served as an Assistant Professor at CV Raman Global University and contributed significantly to student development and academic programs. She has also worked as a teaching assistant at IIT Guwahati and mentored M.Tech. students in their thesis work. She qualified GATE 2016 and UGC-NET 2018 and was awarded the MHRD Fellowship for her Ph.D. Additionally, she has received travel grants to attend prestigious conferences such as ICDCN 2022, COMSNETS 2024, and ICSTE 2023.
She has been actively involved in academic leadership, volunteering at conferences and workshops at IIT Guwahati.

Research Projects
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Adaptive Cell Scheduling Function of 6TiSCH Network for Efficient Data Communication in Industrial IoT
Sponsor: Science & Engineering Research Board (SERB), Govt. of India (under SRG scheme)
Duration: Dec’2019 - Jun’2022 (As part of my Ph.D. thesis work)
Designation: Senior Research Fellow
Project Overview: This project aims to develop enhanced link scheduling functions for 6TiSCH networks for efficient management of TSCH cells used in data communication, focusing on enhancing the network's performance in the context of IIoT.
Principal Investigator: Dr. Manas Khatua, IIT Guwahati
Publications: ICDCN’22, ICSTE’23, COMSNETS’24, Elsevier ADHOC’24
Security-by-Design in IoT: Securing IoT Protocols against Cyber Attacks in Industrial IoT Applications
Sponsor: Science & Engineering Research Board (SERB), Govt. of India (under CRG Scheme)
Duration: Dec’2024 - Jun’2027 (As part of my Ph.D. thesis work)
Designation: Senior Research Fellow
Project Overview: The main goal is to investigate and design security schemes for 6TiSCH IoT protocols. Often, IoT protocol designs overlook security during control message exchanges, creating vulnerabilities that can be exploited. Therefore, incorporating security into the design of each protocol in 6TiSCH networks is crucial. Specifically, I aim to secure the link scheduler MSF and the associated 6P protocol.
Principal Investigator: Dr. Manas Khatua, IIT Guwahati
Publications: INDICON’23
