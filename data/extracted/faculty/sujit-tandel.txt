Research Interests
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Spectroscopy of metastable states in transuranic, superheavy elements
Isomeric states in atomic nuclei arising from different excitation mechanisms
Diverse nuclear shapes: prolate, oblate and octupole deformed
Instrumentation and measurement techniques for basic and applied research
New approaches for multi-parameter data reduction and analysis of nuclear spectroscopic data
Nuclear model calculations for deformed rare-earth and actinide nuclei
Exploration of nuclear energy sources across India through gamma-ray spectroscopy
Identification of chemical composition through x-ray fluorescence studies
