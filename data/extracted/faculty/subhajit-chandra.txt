Biography
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dr. Subhajit Chandra holds a Ph.D. in Design from IIT Guwahati. He is currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Design, School of Humanities and Social Sciences at Shiv Nadar University. His research interests include non-Latin typography and type design, reading psychology, cognitive studies in visual design, and new media technology and design. Among his achievements, he received a consultancy project titled 'Bilingual Branding: Redesigning of Brand Identity' from Khemka Global Infrastructure Pvt Ltd. He is also teaching as a Visiting Faculty at NID Andra Pradesh.
