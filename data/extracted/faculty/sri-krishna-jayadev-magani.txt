Faculty at School of Natural Sciences

Research Interest
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
My research interest is in understanding the cancer cell signaling in disease progression and therapy. Presently working on Understanding the mechanism of Drug resistance development to multi-kinase inhibitor drugs in colorectal cancers. other interests include understanding the signaling in some rare tropical diseases.
