================================================================================
Rahul Jain
================================================================================

Faculty at School of Humanities and Social Sciences

Biography
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rahul Jain is an economist and a development practitioner with expertise in just sustainable food systems transformations. He has worked with some of India's largest and most innovative organisations like Samaj Pragati Sahayog and the Chief Minister's Good Governance Associates programme to deliver effective governance for social justice.

Education
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Masters in Public Administration (MPA) in Innovation, Public Policy, and Public Value from Institute for Innovation and Public Purpose, University College London
Post Graduate Diploma in Liberal Studies (Young India Fellowship) from Ashoka University
Bachelor of Arts (Honours Course) in Economics from University of Delhi

Work Experience
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Deputy Manager, Chief Minister’s Good Governance Associates
Fundraising, Donor Relations and Reporting Officer, Samaj Pragati Sahayog
