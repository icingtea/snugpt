*Subject: Launch of SBI Platinum Jubilee Asha Scholarship Programme 2025 –
Reg.*

The *SBI Foundation*, the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) arm of
the *State
Bank of India*, has launched its flagship initiative titled *“SBI Platinum
Jubilee Asha Scholarship Programme 2025.”*
This significant programme aims to *empower 23,230 meritorious students
from underprivileged backgrounds across India*, enabling them to pursue
their academic aspirations with dignity and confidence.

Under this programme, *financial assistance ranging from ₹15,000 to
₹20,00,000 per annum* will be provided to eligible students *from Class 9
up to postgraduate levels*, until completion of their respective courses.

*Categories Covered:*

   - *School Students:* Class 9 to 12
   - *Undergraduate Students:* Enrolled in institutions ranked up to *NIRF
   300* or accredited with *NAAC Grade A*
   - *MBBS Students:* Enrolled in institutions ranked up to *NIRF 300* or
   accredited with *NAAC Grade A*
   - *Postgraduate Students:* Enrolled in institutions ranked up to *NIRF
   300* or accredited with *NAAC Grade A*
   - *Scholars at IITs and IIMs*
   - *SC/ST Students:* Pursuing Master’s or higher education abroad at *Top
   200 QS-ranked universities*

Out of the total *23,230 beneficiaries*,

   - *20,000 students* will be supported under the *School category* across
   all States and Union Territories, and
   - *3,230 students* will be supported at *Undergraduate and Postgraduate
   levels.*

      All *institutions* are requested to:

   1. *Disseminate* this information among their students through notice
   boards, institutional websites, and social media platforms.
   2. *Encourage eligible students* to apply for the scholarship under the
   appropriate category.
   3. Provide necessary guidance and documentation support to applicants,
   wherever required.

For detailed eligibility criteria, application process, and timelines,
institutions and students may visit the official *SBI Foundation website*
 at:

https://www.sbiashascholarship.co.in

This initiative is an excellent opportunity to support the academic
progression of deserving students and to contribute towards inclusive
education in India.
