*Eligibility Requirements:  *Any student with relevant Food Affairs
Committee experience and a CGPA above 6.50 shall be eligible.

*Role: *The roles of the Secretary will be as follows:

   1. Present and report the Committee proceedings to the Council.
   2. Be responsible for implementing suggestions and feedback from the
   Council and student body.
   3. Ensure weekly meetings with Committee heads take place.
   4. Address the Senate at the beginning of their tenure and outline the
   agenda for the upcoming year.
   5. Appoint the Chairperson as the de facto deputy and head of the Core
   Committee.
   6. Actively look for ways and means to improve (1) Quality and (2)
   Availability of food.
