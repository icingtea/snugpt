*These Instructions Will Come Into Effect from 9 AM on 19th November *
Dear Students,
As per University regulations you are expected to carry your physical ID as
well and present it to any University authority on demand.
However, it has been agreed to permit the use of your personal DigiiCampus
ID on your mobile phone as proof of identity to enter the Library premises.
You may show this or your Physical ID Card for verification to enter the
Library.
*Note*: *This approval ONLY APPLIES TO ENTERING THE LIBRARY. *
For other purposes, such as taking sports equipment on loan, borrowing
books from the Library, or entering hostels other than your own, or when
asked to establish your identity by University authorities, you will still
NEED TO SHOW / DEPOSIT YOUR PHYSICAL ID CARD.
