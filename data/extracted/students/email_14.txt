Dear Students,

The University will go into recess after the Monsoon Semester 2025, from
16th December 2025.

Please note the following important information:

1.    The Mess (DH) will continue to run as is. In case of low footfall,
one of the DH may be shut down subsequently.


2.      *No student is allowed to stay on campus after 16th December,
without a request from the concerned School or Department,* stating the
purpose of stay, after which this will need to be approved by the Dean of
Students. Students are required to obtain the necessary *approval on the
attached form, which needs to be downloaded, filled, countersigned by
respective Faculty / HoD/ Dean and submitted to the Office of the Dean of
Students latest by 12th December *2025. (*This does not apply to resident
PhD Scholars*).


3.       *Students who have booked flight/train tickets for 17th December*,
need to approach their hostel wardens, who are authorised to grant
permission to stay in the hostel for an extra day.


4.    For students permitted to stay on campus during the semester break,
the rules of conduct for hostels and going off campus, applicable during
the semester, will continue to apply. *Any student found breaking the
rules, or involved in any act of indiscipline will be asked to leave Campus
immediately*, apart from whatever disciplinary action might be attracted
for any offence(s) committed.


5.    *Students staying back* on Campus during the Semester Break *must be
present and punctual for the project work, sports practice, or other
purpose *that justifies their staying back, failing which, their permission
may be revoked and they will be required to leave.


The classes for Spring Semester 2026 will start on *12th January 2026* and
the I/E grade exams are scheduled from 8th to 10th January. *Students may
return to campus after the Winter Break on or after Friday, 9th January
2026*. Those who have I/E grade exams are permitted to return on Thursday,
7th January 2026.


With best wishes to all of you for your end semester exams and for the
vacation ahead*.*
