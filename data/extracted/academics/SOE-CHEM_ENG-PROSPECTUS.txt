Chemical Engineering B.Tech. (UG) Curriculum 2023-27
For award of B.Tech. degree in chemical engineering, a student is required to earn a
minimum of 160 credits by completing courses in the curriculum. This document lists
these courses category-wise and semester-wise. Once admitted into this programme, the
students should plan their progress according to these guidelines.
